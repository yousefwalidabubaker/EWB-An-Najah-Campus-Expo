About EWB Campus Expo 2025.

EWB Campus Expo 2025 was held at An-Najah National University on 20 March 2025 by EWB An-Najah.

★ ✦ ★ ✦ ★

The expo

A space for student projects, engineering ideas, and community-focused work to be shared with visitors across campus.

Visitors can explore booths, follow the event schedule, and reserve seats for the expo.

03

Student teams can present their work, connect with visitors, and request a booth for their project.
